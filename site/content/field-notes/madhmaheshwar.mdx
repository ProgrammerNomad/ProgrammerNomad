## Route

Multi-day trek through Garhwal with steady altitude gain. Base logistics typically from Ukhimath.

## Costs

Guesthouses and guides vary by season; carry cash for remote stops.

## Best time

Pre-monsoon and post-monsoon windows for clearer views and safer trails.
