## What it does

JodKit packages opinionated Laravel modules-auth, billing hooks, admin patterns-so teams ship faster without reinventing scaffolding.

## Why I built it

After years of greenfield Laravel projects, the same ADR decisions kept repeating. JodKit captures those patterns as reusable modules.

## Stack

- Laravel 11+
- PHP 8.2+
- Modular service providers and domain boundaries
