# Globe geography

`world-rings.json` is generated by `node scripts/prepare-globe.mjs` from Natural Earth v5.1.2, 1:110m admin-0 country polygons, revision `f1890d9f152c896d250a77557a5751a93d494776`.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/f1890d9f152c896d250a77557a5751a93d494776/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson

Public-domain terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Boundaries follow the dataset's default de facto representation, not a claim of sovereignty: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/disputed-boundaries-policy/

Outer rings are simplified with a 0.25-degree Douglas–Peucker tolerance and rounded to two decimals. Small islands, interior lakes and fine border detail are intentionally omitted at this scale. The geometry is illustrative and is separate from the site's unchanged recorded destination coordinates. Both WebGL and static poster use the same rings. No geographic service or added library is required at runtime.
